How does it look?

Kids have a special ability to learn and adapt quickly to new situations, which is why they are able to learn a new language a lot faster than a grown-up.

They can use this ability to their own advantage, if they are encouraged to develop their skills and mental capacity.

That is why we offer courses, where we teach children the language of coding.

This new and exciting opportunity will not just help children to learn coding for their potential future, but also teach the ability to think logically and adjust to new technological surroundings fast.

We approach a learning through play method as this is proven to be the most effective!

What the kids will be using:

During the classes we will provide some of the most modern technologies:

Encoding mats

The mats help children gain or enhance the ability to think logically at every step of the process.

Games

We offer playful, interactive board games that encourage children to activate their brain as well as the body.

Blocks

The blocks provide hands-on, screenless plays, which help to learn the basics of coding without even realizing it.

Robots

These screenless games are designed to help children visualize the basics of coding, while providing endless opportunities to create something new.

Puzzles

These little robots can follow any self-made code, by moving according to color coordinated pathways, play music sequences or even games.
